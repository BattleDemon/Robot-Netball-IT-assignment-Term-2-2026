
    • Robots must operate fully autonomously, No remote control or human input after start or moving to foul box
    • Playing Field: 158 cm by 219 cm, and made of a green carpet or similar
    • Division of field:  The field will be divided into three equal section along the long side by a strip of black tape
    • Foul areas: there will be a foul area at right hand corner of each short side. The foul area will be white
    • Hoops:  A 30cm high hoop is to be placed in the middle of the short sides
    • Ball must pass fully through the hoop to score
    • 1 point per goal
    • No ramming or pinning, to do so is a foul
    • Holding the ball without progress for more than 10 seconds is a foul
    • While holding the ball robot must have one wheel still as “pivot”
    • A robot that commits a foul will be picked up and placed in a foul box and must stay there for 5 seconds
    • Maximum size: 30 × 30 x 30 cm at any point of operation
    • Any EV3 sensors allowed
